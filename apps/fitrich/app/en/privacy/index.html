Fitrich privacy policy

Updated October 5, 2026.

Summary

Nothing you record in Fitrich leaves your device. There is no account, no server of ours holding data and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

Weights, workouts, transactions, goals and preferences live in your device's own storage (the browser's or the app's IndexedDB and localStorage). The project does not receive, see or recover this data.

What Fitrich does not do

It asks for no sign-up, e-mail, phone number or location. It uses no ads, analytics, third-party cookies or third-party SDK. It makes no network requests while you use it: the site's security policy (connect-src 'none') makes the browser block any outgoing request.

Permissions

In the app installed from a store, the only optional permission is notifications, used only for local reminders you turn on (for example, logging the day's spending). They are scheduled on the device itself, with no server.

Backup and export

You decide if, when and where to export a copy of your data. The backup can be encrypted with a password only you know; without it, nobody can open the file. If you lose the device and have no backup, the data cannot be recovered.

Erase everything

The Data screen has a button that erases all data on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data also erases everything.

Children

Fitrich is a personal tracking tool and is not directed at children under 13. Since it collects no data, there is no child data to handle.

Notice

Fitrich is not medical, nutritional, financial or investment advice.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes. This is the source-code policy; whoever publishes Fitrich at their own address should add their contact here.
